# QA & Safety charter

## Shared authority and current scope

Use the [project brief](../project-brief.md) and [decision log](../decision-log.md). Workstream owner, operational response owner, and acceptance authority are unresolved (Q-08, Q-14). Current scope includes validating the website concept and documenting future launch gates (D-008–D-010). Product safety policies and service operations remain unresolved.

## Responsibilities

Make approved requirements verifiable and identify risks across signup, matching, notifications, data handling, logistics, and the 1:1 date experience. Surface decisions needed for consent, eligibility, reporting, blocking, support, accessibility, and exception handling. Propose safeguards for approval rather than asserting unapproved policies or promising safety outcomes.

## Initial deliverables after authorization

- A requirements-to-checks map covering happy paths and exceptions, including the exclusion of group events.
- A risk and open-policy register covering privacy, unwanted disclosure, inappropriate matching, harassment, operational failures, cancellations, and accessibility needs.
- Proposed acceptance scenarios and escalation responsibilities, explicitly labeling unsettled policies and ownership.
- A readiness checklist distinguishing verified evidence, untested assumptions, unresolved risks, and decisions requiring approval.

## Dependencies

Eligibility, matching, reveal, logistics, privacy, support, exceptions, and success thresholds require direction (Q-01, Q-04–Q-12). Design supplies approved journeys; Engineering supplies technical constraints and later implementation evidence; Growth & Experiences supplies approved operational procedures. Any jurisdiction-specific review depends on approved geography and scope. This charter establishes no legal conclusion or policy.

## Completion criteria

Authorized deliverables map to the shared requirements, include negative and exception scenarios, identify owners or explicitly unresolved ownership, and receive explicit acceptance from the user or a delegated approver. Readiness claims must state their evidence and limits. Unresolved risk acceptance must be recorded as a decision; a checklist alone cannot authorize launch or operations.

## Website-specific handoff

Use [validation and launch gates](../validation-and-launch-gates.md). Report executed checks separately from planned checks, including keyboard and reduced-motion behavior, small-screen layout, navigation/modal recovery, no unintended data collection, and truthful concept messaging. A working prototype is not evidence of matching quality or operational readiness.
