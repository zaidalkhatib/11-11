# Growth & Experiences charter

## Shared authority and current scope

Use the [project brief](../project-brief.md) and [decision log](../decision-log.md). Workstream owner and acceptance authority are unresolved (Q-14). Website concept positioning may support the authorized build (D-008–D-009). Outreach, service launch, booking, and spending remain outside this phase.

## Responsibilities

Develop proposals for positioning, acquisition, curated dinner/activity experiences, anticipation, and service logistics consistent with exclusively 1:1 blind dates. Coordinate promises and communications with Design, operating requirements with Engineering, and exception handling and support with QA & Safety. Do not present matching as proven or guaranteed without evidence.

## Initial deliverables after authorization

- An audience and positioning proposal, distinguishing confirmed intent from audience, channel, and commercial assumptions.
- An experience concept outline for two people, with proposed selection criteria and open accessibility, dietary, scheduling, and operational questions.
- A service outline covering signup through date follow-up, marking unresolved notification, reveal, cancellation, and support decisions.
- A proposed measurement plan for acquisition, experience quality, and repeat interest, coordinated with shared success and safety criteria.

## Dependencies

Audience, geography, signup commitments, reveal timing, logistics ownership, commercial model, safety, exceptions, outcomes, and acquisition remain open (Q-01–Q-08, Q-10–Q-12, Q-15). The live reference replaces the missing-screenshot dependency (D-008); final original identity acceptance remains open. Any external research requiring contact, vendor commitments, reservations, paid activity, or publication requires explicit authorization.

## Completion criteria

Authorized deliverables remain exclusively 1:1, distinguish concepts from available or booked experiences, make dependencies and costs explicit as unresolved where applicable, and receive explicit acceptance from the user or a delegated approver. Proposed claims, channels, pricing, partners, and operating procedures must not be treated as approved. Completion does not authorize a launch, outreach, purchases, or bookings.
