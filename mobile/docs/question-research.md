# Relationship-process question rationale

These four optional, original option-based prompts complement the existing 84-step questionnaire. Each is editable under Love & connection. They ask about desired interaction, not about symptoms, attachment labels, or a partner’s past behavior. No real matching or scoring is implemented.

| Key | Gap addressed | Primary research |
|---|---|---|
| goodNewsSupport | Receiving positive-event support, distinct from existing hard-day support | Gable et al. (2004), [What do you do when things go right?](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15301629/) |
| feelingUnderstood | Behaviors that communicate responsiveness, distinct from how quickly trust grows | Laurenceau et al. (1998), [Intimacy as an interpersonal process](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9599440/) |
| conflictRepair | Reconnecting after disagreement, distinct from when to begin discussing it | Salvatore et al. (2011), [Recovering from conflict in romantic relationships](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0956797610397055) |
| goalSupport | Support that preserves the other person’s agency, distinct from ambition or solo time | Feeney & Thrush (2010), [Relationship influences on exploration in adulthood](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20053031/) |

## Evidence boundaries
The cited studies investigate relationship processes, generally among existing partners. Association with intimacy or relationship well-being does not establish that a dating-app answer predicts attraction, compatibility, or future success. These original items are not validated versions of the instruments used in those studies. Responses are context-dependent self-reports; none is labeled healthy/unhealthy or assigned a ranking. Optional omission and context-dependent choices are supported. Do not use equal answers as an automatic match criterion.

The existing questionnaire already covers broad values, lifestyle, faith, family plans, social energy, communication, conflict timing, and goals. Only these four incremental domains were added rather than duplicating those topics. Existing astrology and self-rated looks questions are preferences/self-description, not scientifically validated matching signals.
